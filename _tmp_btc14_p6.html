EXPERIMENTS TO RUN

1. On the channel tab: pay Bob three times, then select the very first (50/50) state and broadcast it — watch the log show your own simulator recomputing the shachain secret and detecting the cheat. 2. Broadcast the current (unrevoked) latest state instead — notice there's no penalty, just a normal delayed settlement. 3. On the routing tab: drain the channel directly between the two cheapest-route nodes, then find a route again — watch Dijkstra fall back to a longer, more expensive path, and notice the total CLTV cost it now carries. 4. Send a payment, then open the console-style log and check that the destination's revealed preimage actually satisfies SHA256(R) === H at every hop before the simulator lets the HTLC settle.
